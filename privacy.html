Privacy Notice — Beta

This is a starter Beta notice and should be reviewed before a commercial/public launch.

English Haters uses account information to provide learning progress, profile features, device management and content access.

Data used

  • Name and email for student accounts.
  • Optional profile picture.
  • Practice answers, XP, streak/activity days, mistakes and exam history.
  • A random browser/device identifier and basic browser/platform label for the two-device limit.

Why it is used

To authenticate students, sync progress, personalize learning, detect weak topics, manage device access and operate the Beta.

Profile photos

Profile photos are optional. In local mode they stay in that browser. In cloud mode they are stored with the student's account.

Student choices

Students can remove their profile picture and sign out. Account deletion/support procedures should be added before paid launch.

Contact

Before public launch, replace this section with the official English Haters support/contact address and business identity.
