# Articles — Written Practice Worksheets

**Learning track:** SSC  
**Topic slug:** `article`  
**Source alignment:** NEED, *Basic English Grammar*, Article chapter, printed pp. 218–241.  
**Content status:** Original practice written for English-Hater; not copied from the source exercises.

Use **A**, **An**, **The** or **×** (no article) where appropriate.

## Level 1 — Foundation

1. My brother wants to become ___ engineer.
2. We saw ___ owl in the garden.
3. She studies at ___ university near her home.
4. ___ moon looked bright last night.
5. ___ students need regular practice.
6. Please give me ___ glass of water.
7. He plays ___ football on Fridays.
8. I have ___ few questions about the lesson.
9. We waited for ___ hour.
10. ___ information in this notice is important.

## Level 2 — Application

1. Many ___ student has asked the teacher for extra practice.
2. We visited ___ Netherlands during our study tour.
3. She bought ___ dozen notebooks for the class.
4. ___ more you read, ___ more vocabulary you learn.
5. My aunt plays ___ violin, but I prefer ___ cricket.
6. He gave me ___ useful piece of advice.
7. ___ rich should not ignore ___ poor.
8. The headteacher went to ___ school to inspect the new laboratory.
9. It was ___ most interesting lecture I attended that week.
10. What ___ unusual question!

## Level 3 — Context and exceptions

### Passage A

___ Padma is one of the major rivers of Bangladesh. ___ river carries a large volume of water during the rainy season. People living beside it depend on ___ river for transport and fishing. ___ water in some areas rises quickly during heavy rain.

### Passage B

Rafi wants to become ___ architect. He studies at ___ university in Dhaka. He usually has ___ breakfast before leaving home. On his way, he listens to ___ English news programme. He believes that ___ more he practises, ___ more confident he will become.

### Passage C

___ honesty is important in public life. A public servant should serve people fairly. ___ officer who abuses power can damage public trust. We should respect ___ honest people who work in this institution and learn from their examples.

## Answer key with brief explanations

### Level 1

1. **an** — engineer begins with a vowel sound.
2. **an** — owl begins with a vowel sound.
3. **a** — university begins with /j/.
4. **The** — the moon is treated as unique in this context.
5. **×** — general plural noun.
6. **a** — glass is a countable unit.
7. **×** — sport name used generally.
8. **a** — a few means some.
9. **an** — hour begins with a vowel sound.
10. **The** — the phrase in this notice identifies specific information.

### Level 2

1. **a** — many a + singular noun takes a singular verb.
2. **the** — the Netherlands.
3. **a** — a dozen notebooks.
4. **The, the** — paired comparative structure.
5. **the, ×** — the violin; cricket as a sport.
6. **a** — a useful piece; useful begins with /j/.
7. **The, the** — group expressions.
8. **the** — a particular school building is meant.
9. **a** — most means “very” in this sentence.
10. **an** — unusual begins with a vowel sound.

### Level 3

**Passage A:** The, The, the, The. The first identifies the river by name; later references are specific.

**Passage B:** an, a, ×, an, the, the. Architect begins with a vowel sound; university begins with /j/; breakfast is general; English begins with a vowel sound; the last two blanks form a paired comparative.

**Passage C:** ×, An, the. Honesty is general and abstract; an officer is a singular countable noun used generally; the honest people who work in this institution is a specific group.
