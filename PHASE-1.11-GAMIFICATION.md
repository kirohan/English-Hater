# English Haters — Phase 1.11 Gamification & Retention

This phase adds a retention layer designed around genuine learning behavior rather than artificial points.

## Student retention system

- Daily mission: 5 answers.
- Three dynamic daily missions.
- Three weekly missions.
- XP and named level tiers.
- 13 permanent achievements.
- Achievement unlock celebrations on practice/exam results.
- 28-day consistency heatmap.
- Next-milestone progress card.
- Achievement progress shown on Home and English Passport.
- Earned achievements persist even if a streak later breaks or accuracy later changes.
- Achievement IDs are synced through `user_progress.achievements_json`.

## Current level titles

- Starter
- Builder
- Challenger
- Scholar
- Master
- Legend

The underlying XP rule is unchanged: practice correct = +10 XP, practice wrong = +2 XP, timed exam correct = +5 XP.

## Achievement principles

Badges are derived from meaningful activity such as answering, streaks, exams, mastery, accuracy, and clearing mistakes. There are no purchasable or random rewards.
